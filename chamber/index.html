Who We Are

The Entebbe Chamber of Commerce has supported local businesses, from family-owned shops to growing companies, through networking events, advocacy, and community patnerships. Wether youre a long time resident or just opening your doors, we,re here to help you build relationships and reach more customers.
Our Membership levels(Non-profit, Bronze, Silver and Gold) offer benefits for every business size, inclusing event access, promotional opportunities and directory listings

  • Representation Advocacy on local zoning, tax and permit decisions
  • Workforce Patnerships with schools and training programs
  • Regulatory Relief Streamlinde licensing and fewer compliance burdens
  • Community Commerce Shop-local campaigns that grow member campaigns
  • 150+ Member businesses
  • 40+ Events annually
  • 5+ Years serving Entebbe City
  • 5000+ Local jobs supported
